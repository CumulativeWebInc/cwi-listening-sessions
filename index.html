WEEKLY · FRIDAYS 8 PM ET

CWI Lab: Signal Sessions

Working title — Black picks the final name.

The weekly show where hosts Marcus and Nia spin a track in full and run it through the CWI tool bench live on the air — the Trust Verdict Checker, an audio quality report (loudness/peaks), and a lyric sheet generator. Five acts: cold open, the spin, the lab, the verdict, the call to action.

Setlists come from the Friday 8 PM ET Listening Room sessions. Artists: submit your track to get played in full and run through the lab live — every submitter joins the CWI Tester Network.
