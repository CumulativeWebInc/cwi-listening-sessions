# Distribution — CWI Listening Room: Weekly Sessions

**Stable feed URL (paste this everywhere):**
`https://cumulativewebinc.github.io/cwi-listening-sessions/feed.xml`

**Why this exists:** permanent GitHub Pages URLs (never expiring) so directories can index the show. Submit once per directory below.

**Indexing time:** directories take 24–72 hours to review and index after submission. The show will NOT appear in Spotify search until review completes — that is normal, not a failure.

---

## Spotify — Spotify for Podcasters (Black taps this, ~2 min)

**Do this AFTER approving the pilot episode** (the feed needs at least one episode to submit).

1. Open https://podcasters.spotify.com → log in with your Spotify account.
2. Click **Add a podcast** (or the `+`).
3. Paste the feed URL above when asked for the RSS feed → **Next**.
4. Verify ownership (Spotify shows a code to confirm — follow the on-screen step).
5. Review the show details → **Submit**.
6. Done. New Friday episodes appear in Spotify automatically — no repeat step.

## Apple Podcasts — Apple Podcasts Connect (optional, ~2 min)

1. Open https://podcastsconnect.apple.com → sign in with your Apple ID.
2. Click the **+** → **New Show** → **Add a show with an RSS feed**.
3. Paste the feed URL above → **Add**.
4. Review validation → **Publish**.

- Episodic, `audio/mpeg` enclosures, byte lengths + durations in every item.
- Cover: 1600×1600 JPG (meets Apple's 1400×1400 minimum).
- All episodes marked `itunes:explicit: no`.
