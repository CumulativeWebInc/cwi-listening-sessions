Submit Your Track to the Lab

CWI Lab: Signal Sessions (working title) is the weekly show where we spin a track in full and run it through the CWI tool bench — live, on the air: the Trust Verdict Checker, the audio quality report (loudness/peaks), and the lyric sheet generator.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below and hit Send submission. It opens your email app with everything pre-filled — addressed to CWI.
  2. If your track is picked for the show, we reply with an upload inbox for your master file.
  3. On the episode: we play your track in full, run the lab tools on it live, and scorecard the results. Machine-draft lyrics stay labeled draft until a human corrects them.
  4. Every submitting artist joins the CWI Tester Network — front-row testing of every CWI tool we build. Testers first, customers later.

Consent & terms (short, on purpose)

By submitting, you confirm:

  • You own or control the rights to the track you're submitting.
  • You grant Cumulative Web Inc permission to play the track in full on CWI Lab: Signal Sessions (audio podcast + video cut).
  • You grant CWI permission to run its lab tools on the track on the air — loudness/peak analysis, machine-draft lyric transcription, and trust/history checks where applicable — and to publish the tool outputs as part of the episode.
  • Your masters stay yours. We play them, we measure them, we don't claim them.
  • No payment is promised for the submission or the play. This is a demo bench, not a licensing deal.
